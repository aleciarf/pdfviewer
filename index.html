PDF Flip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

PDF Flip is a PDF flipbook reader maintained by the site owner. It supports local PDFs, reading progress, page links, attached audio and optional Google Drive storage.

Google access and purpose

When you sign in, the app uses your Google account identifier, name, email and profile information to identify your account and separate its device storage. It requests permission to create and access app-created Drive files (drive.file) to save your personal PDFs, attached audio, folder names, links and reading progress. It creates a “DCD Book Library” folder and a library.json file in your Drive. This data is used to provide the reader and library features you choose.

Loading the optional shared default library asks separately for broader read-only Drive access (drive.readonly), because the shared folder was not created by the app. With that permission, the app reads that configured shared folder and your app library; its code does not scan your unrelated Drive files. Google may require additional consent and verification for this optional permission.

Storage and sharing

PDFs, audio, account-specific library metadata and reading progress are cached in IndexedDB on your device. Theme, autosave preferences and an optional public Client ID override are stored in browser local storage. Guest storage and each Google account use separate databases. Cached account data remains after sign-out; someone who can access your browser storage may be able to read it. Google access tokens are held in page memory, not stored by this app in browser storage.

Private uploads and library metadata are sent directly from your browser to Google Drive. The app does not send those files to an app-owner server or make them public. File visibility follows the permissions in your Drive. Google handles authentication and storage under its Privacy Policy. Cloudflare hosts this site and CDN providers deliver the PDF reader code; providers may process IP addresses and technical request information. The app includes no advertising or separate analytics tracker.

Limited Use

PDF Flip’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Google data is used only for these visible library features. It is not sold, used for advertising or used to train AI models.

Control, retention and deletion

Autosave can be disabled in Settings. Save library uploads your library; Load from Drive can replace this account’s device library after confirmation. Import guest books copies this device’s guest PDFs and attachments into the connected account’s library, which can then be uploaded.

Sign out clears the current app connection but keeps Drive files and device caches; it does not sign you out of Google or revoke permissions. Delete “DCD Book Library” in Drive and empty its trash to permanently remove saved cloud files. Clear this site’s data in browser settings on every device to remove cached data. Revoke permissions at Google Account → Third-party connections. Deleting a book in the app removes it from the library, not its original Drive PDF or previously uploaded audio. Account data stays until you remove it through these controls.

Contact and supervised use

For support or privacy requests, use the support email shown on PDF Flip’s Google consent screen. Do not share private PDFs or credentials in public support channels. Adults should supervise children and manage any connected Google account in accordance with Google’s account rules. This page will be updated when data handling changes.
